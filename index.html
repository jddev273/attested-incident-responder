01 / The problem

Cross-chain risk has a permission lag.

A reserve condition can deteriorate on one chain while treasury spending authority remains unchanged on another. AIR closes that gap with a verified response path instead of a chat message, dashboard alert, or model instruction.

One concrete incident
A protected reserve falls below its configured threshold.

The source condition is objective. AIR does not need to invent an attacker, diagnose an exploit, or guess why the balance moved.

01
Observe and trigger

An operator or guardian observes the protected-balance condition and starts evaluation.

02
Verify before authority changes

Finalized Ethereum evidence reaches the proof / attestation path. Stale or future evidence fails closed.

03
Apply the floor; optionally strengthen

Deterministic policy sets the minimum restriction. For a WARNING, bounded AI may recommend an equal-or-stricter mode; an authorized signer must bind that strengthening to the evidence.

04
Enforce until recovery is proved

Creditcoin contracts constrain the vault. Returning to NORMAL requires a separate verified recovery proof; the model cannot clear containment.

03 / Bounded AI

If the model disappears, containment still holds.

The safety property does not depend on model availability or obedience. AI is a discretionary strengthening layer above deterministic policy, not the root of custody or recovery authority.

Deterministic invariant

The model can make a response stricter. It cannot make it weaker.

Authenticated evidence and source-derived severity define the minimum restriction. Invalid, unavailable, or weaker model output falls back to that floor.

effective mode = max(deterministic floor, bounded recommendation)
Failure behavior

Useful judgment, no sovereign authority.

Model offline? Base containment remains available.
Malformed output? Ignore it and preserve the floor.
Requests weaker mode? Reject the weakening.
Wants recovery? Impossible without separate proof.
Chooses beneficiary or calldata? Outside model authority.

07 / Authority boundary

Useful AI. Deliberately limited authority.

AIR separates verified evidence, bounded model judgment, responder authorization, treasury enforcement, and recovery so no model response becomes unilateral custody authority.

What AIR demonstrates

  • Cross-chain evidence is required before destination action.
  • Spending restrictions are economically enforced on Creditcoin CC3.
  • A deterministic minimum restriction survives bad or unavailable model output.
  • Recovery is a separate verified proof event.
  • Core public receipts can be inspected without a wallet or model credential.

Authority kept outside the model

  • Cannot sign or broadcast treasury actions.
  • Cannot choose beneficiaries, destinations, or calldata.
  • Cannot weaken the deterministic containment floor.
  • Cannot invent proof or replace verified source evidence.
  • Cannot authorize recovery; a separate verified recovery event is required.

Verified evidence should change what money is allowed to do.

AIR makes that policy transition explicit, enforceable, and inspectable - while keeping AI useful without making it sovereign.
